--- layout: default title: Abram Pérez Herrero --- # Abram Pérez Herrero I am a PhD student in Cosmology and Astrophysics, funded by an FPI grant from the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC). I completed both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees at the University of Cantabria, where my thesis work focused on the search for dark matter, specifically exploring dark matter spike populations and primordial black holes. Since beginning my PhD in March 2024 under the supervision of Diego Herranz and Rafael Guzmán, my research has centered on the ARRAKIHS space mission. ## Research Interests My research focuses on the use of stellar streams as precision probes of galaxy evolution and the nature of dark matter. Stellar streams, as remnants of tidally disrupted systems, retain detailed dynamical information about the assembly history of galaxies and the underlying gravitational potential. By modeling their morphology, density fluctuations, and kinematics, I aim to extract constraints on the Milky Way’s mass distribution and on the small-scale structure of dark matter. In particular, I am interested in: - Using stellar stream perturbations to test predictions of different dark matter models beyond standard ΛCDM. - Constraining the subhalo population through stream–substructure interactions. - Exploring the connection between hierarchical galaxy formation and stream properties. In addition, I have contributed to complementary research projects, including: - Simulation of cosmic ray contamination to generate realistic mock images for the ARRAKIHS mission. - Analysis of dark matter spike populations using black hole evolution models. --- ## Publications An updated list of publications can be found in the following databases: --- ## CV --- ## Contact Abram Pérez Herrero Instituto de Física de Cantabria (IFCA) Santander, Spain
