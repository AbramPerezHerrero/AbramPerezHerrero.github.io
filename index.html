--- layout: default title: Abram Pérez Herrero --- # Abram Pérez Herrero I am a PhD student in Cosmology and Astrophysics, funded by an FPI grant from the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC). I completed both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees at the University of Cantabria, where my thesis work focused on dark matter searches, specifically investigating dark matter spike populations and primordial black holes. Since March 2024, I have been pursuing my PhD at the Instituto de Física de Cantabria (IFCA) under the supervision of Diego Herranz and Rafael Guzmán.
